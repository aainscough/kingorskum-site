About Luthinor Games

We're the small independent Roblox studio that makes King or Skum?. Here's why we built a game where everyone wants to dethrone the King.

Luthinor Games is a small independent studio making games on Roblox. King or Skum? is our first release, launched in October 2026.

Why we made King or Skum?

The game started with a card game. In Scum (also called President), the winner of each round becomes King and the loser becomes Scum, and the next round deals the King the best cards. It's unfair on purpose, and it's the most fun anyone has at the table, because everyone is trying to knock the King off.

We wanted that feeling in an adventure game: one crown per server, a King who really is stronger and richer than everyone else, and a whole realm of players who want what he has. Everything else, from the King's Tax to Anarchy to civil wars, grew out of asking "what would the people do?"

How we build

  • Free to play, fair to win. The crown can only be won with renown, and renown can't be bought.
  • Secrets reward curiosity. We hide characters and achievements for players who poke at the edges of the world.
  • We update often and publish every change on our updates page.
