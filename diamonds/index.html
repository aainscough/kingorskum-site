How to Get Diamonds in King or Skum?

Every source of diamonds in King or Skum?, with drop rates, the fastest free methods, and what's actually worth buying.

Last updated October 28, 2026 · by Luthinor Games

Play Free on Roblox

Diamonds are the rarest currency in King or Skum?. You'll see your total under your gold in the top-left corner. Here's every way to earn them, fastest first, and what's worth spending them on.

The fastest free diamonds

  1. Join the group for 30.
  2. Chase cheap achievements, like slaying 100 chickens (3) and becoming the Knight (5).
  3. Run the Molten Maze daily for 5.
  4. Join every dragon raid and stay alive for 3.
  5. Play the Colosseum for up to 5 a day, if you're brave.

What to spend diamonds on

  • Gear buyback (10): after you die, buy back what you were wearing. Only your last death can be bought back.
  • Writ of Absence (10): from Mortimer Tithe by the bank. Protects your gear and renown the next time you're away more than a day.
  • Royal Bank vaults (100 and 1,000): banked gear survives death.
  • Soul binding (50 to 250): at the Soul Shrine for rare, epic and legendary items. Saves the item from one death.
  • Classes (100 to 500): at the White Tower. Owned forever.
  • Atlantis: six shops that take only diamonds.

Diamonds vs. gold

Gold drops from almost everything, is taxed by the King, and is never lost when you die. Diamonds are rare, untaxed and buy the things that protect you from losing everything else. Spend gold freely; spend diamonds on safety.
