A Roblox King of the Hill Game Where the Crown Never Stays Put

A hundred years ago, the black dragon Vyrthrax the Undying burned the old kingdom to the stone. Its lords hid in their towers and let the villages burn. Only one man walked into the Dragon's Maw: a farmhand named Aldric, with a pitchfork and nothing left to lose.

Nobody knows what Aldric said to the dragon. He walked out alive, the dragon went to sleep, and Aldric came home with a crown hammered from the gold the beast had been sleeping on. The people made him King. On his first morning he climbed the castle steps and swore an oath that still binds the realm.

The crown remembers that oath. It slides off the head of any ruler the realm stops talking about and goes looking for whoever is doing the talked-about things: slaying beasts, finishing the impossible quests, toppling Kings. The bards call that fame renown. Everyone else calls it a target.

So in Lionsgate there are only two things you can be. The proverb is older than the castle: you are either King, or you are Skum. Everyone starts at the bottom of that sentence.

How to Play: Rise from Skum to King

Every server is its own kingdom, and every kingdom is a race. Your rank is set by your renown, and your rank makes you stronger or weaker. Nobody stays on top for long.

How to become King →

  2. JackThe King's right hand, and first in line if the King falls.
  3. KnightSword of the court. A bounty hangs on your head.
  4. WorkerHonest blades with ambition.
  5. PeasantMud on your boots, fire in your eyes.
  6. SkumWhere every legend begins. Usually also where it ends.
I

Rise

Start with a rusty sword and a hundred gold. Hunt chickens at Sunnybrook Farm, then goblins, werewolves, raptors and storm titans. Every quest, every kill and every royal you topple earns renown. Climb the ladder fast, because one death sends you back to the bottom.

II

Rule

Earn the most renown and the crown finds you. Command the town guards, proclaim royal decrees, and watch the King's Tax pour into your coffers. The longer you reign, the higher the tax, and the angrier the realm.

III

Fall

Slay the King and you plunder his coffers and plunge Lionsgate into Anarchy: ten minutes of every soul for themselves, after which the most renowned stands crowned. Or whisper to Fennick the coin-flipper and start a civil war from inside the court.

Is King or Skum? free to play?

Yes. King or Skum? is completely free on Roblox. You can earn everything in the game by playing: gold from monsters and quests, diamonds from bosses, fishing and the Colosseum, and every class, vault and piece of gear. Optional Robux packs of gold and diamonds exist to support the game, but the crown can only be won with renown, and renown can't be bought.

What devices can I play King or Skum? on?

Anywhere Roblox runs: Windows PC, Mac, iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets. The game has touch buttons for attacking, blocking and rolling, plus keyboard and mouse controls on computers.

How do I become King in King or Skum?

Earn the most renown on your server. Renown comes from quests, monster kills and slaying other players, especially royals. There are four roads to the throne: slay the King and win the Anarchy that follows, win Anarchy outright, be the Jack when the King falls, or start a civil war as the Jack or Knight. The full breakdown is in our guide How to Become King.

What happens when you die?

You lose half your XP, the gear you're wearing (buy it back for 10 diamonds), your renown, potions, ammo and quest progress. Your gold is always safe, and gear in your Royal Bank vault or bound at the Soul Shrine survives. During Anarchy deaths are gentler: only 20% XP, and monsters can't take your gear.

Is there PvP in King or Skum?

Yes. It's the Crown versus the People: the King, Jack and Knight fight as one team, and every commoner can attack them. Commoners can't hurt each other outside Anarchy and the Colosseum. During Anarchy it's a free-for-all outside the golden safe zones, and the Colosseum is hardcore PvP where the winner takes everything.

Are there King or Skum? codes?

Yes. LONGLIVETHEKING gives 100 diamonds to the first 1,000 players who redeem it. Redeem codes with the Herald, at the Royal Bank or at the Royal Treasury. Three more codes unlock at 100,000, 500,000 and 1,000,000 visits. Every code is on our codes page.

Is it "King or Skum" or "King or Scum"?

The official name is King or Skum?, spelled with a K and a question mark. Lots of players search for King or Scum, which is the same game. The name comes from the card game Scum (also called President), where the winner of each round becomes King and the loser becomes Scum.

Who made King or Skum?

King or Skum? is made by Luthinor Games, a small independent Roblox studio. The game launched in October 2026 and is updated regularly; see updates and patch notes.

Is King or Skum? safe for kids?

King or Skum? has a Mild content maturity label on Roblox, with light, cartoon-style violence (swords, monsters and bosses) and mild fear. In-game chat uses a fixed list of quick-chat phrases like "Hello", "Follow me" and "Good job", with anti-spam limits. Roblox's own parental controls and age settings apply on top of that.
