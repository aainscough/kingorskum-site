How to redeem codes in King or Skum?

  1. Open the Royal Code Scroll in any of three places: talk to the Herald by the fountain and pick "I have a code!"; press Codes at the Royal Bank; or press Codes at the Royal Treasury, where you buy gold and diamonds.
  2. Type the code. Capitals and spaces don't matter.
  3. Press Redeem. Rewards arrive instantly, and each code works once per account.

Milestone codes: unlock them together

Three more codes are already built into the game and switch on by themselves when the realm reaches each visit milestone. The code words are revealed here and in the group the moment each one unlocks. Bring your friends.

What can you spend diamonds on?

  • Buying your gear back after you die (10 diamonds).
  • Classes at the White Tower, from 100 to 500 diamonds.
  • Bigger vaults at the Royal Bank (100 and 1,000 diamonds).
  • A Writ of Absence from Mortimer Tithe (10 diamonds), so you keep your gear if you're away for more than a day.
  • Atlantis, where every shop takes diamonds only.

Other free rewards

  • Achievements: every achievement pays diamonds, from 1 for small talk with a certain dog to 500 for The One True King.
  • Becoming King: every new King is granted 5 diamonds.
  • Slaying the King: the killing blow earns 3 diamonds and half the King's Coffers.
  • Royal Decrees: win the King's Championship (10 diamonds) or catch the Golden Hare (5 diamonds).
