All 11 King or Skum? Bosses, Ranked by Difficulty

Every tyrant in the realm, from Grubnik the Rotten at level 7 to the 60,000-health dragon Vyrthrax, with where to find them and how to win.

Last updated October 28, 2026 · by Luthinor Games

Play Free on Roblox

King or Skum? has 11 bosses, one ruling each land of the realm, plus a raid dragon at the bottom of the world. Below they're ranked from easiest to hardest by level and health. Slay every one, then take the crown without dying once, and you earn The One True King: 500 diamonds and a title.

All 11 bosses, easiest to hardest

#1 · Greenhollow Vale · Goblintown

Grubnik the Rotten

Level 7Health 900

The chief of Goblintown and every adventurer's first boss. Grubnik hits hard for a level 7 and brings his brawlers. He's back about a minute after he falls, so the goblin crate quest stays a fight.

How to beat: Learn his swing. Block or roll the big hit, then punish him while he recovers. Bring a friend or two at level 5 to 7.

#2 · Spider Hollow

The Broodmother

Level 13Health 1,950

Queen of a thousand legs. She lives in the Brood Tree and leaps between her web perches, poisoning anyone who gets close. Ranged attacks do less to her.

How to beat: Bring melee and plenty of healing for the venom. Follow her between perches instead of waiting.

#3 · Hippogryph Highlands

Queen Skyrend

Level 13Health 3,000

Mother of the hippogryphs and thunderbeaks. She swoops from the sky and dives into you, so a fight with her is about timing your roll.

How to beat: Watch for the swoop and roll through it. Fight her on open ground where you can see her coming.

#4 · Spider Hollow · Ashen Caldera

Scorchtail, the Magma King

Level 15Health 3,200

A magma beast who lives in the volcano in Spider Hollow. He doesn't wait around: you'll have to call him, and the quest giver will teach you how.

How to beat: Stay out of the magma. Fire damage adds up fast, and the Pyromaniac class is immune to burning.

#5 · Moonfang Woods

The Moonfang Alpha

Level 19Health 4,000

Leader of the werewolf pack in Moonfang Woods. His howl rallies the pack, and he has to be called before he'll fight.

How to beat: Clear the pack first. A party of three or more at level 17+ makes it manageable.

#6 · Castle Moravek · the crypts

Count Moravek

Level 20Health 6,500

The vampire lord beneath his castle, behind a blood-sealed door at the bottom of the crypts. His Blood Sucker breaks your grip on his treasure chest while he lives.

How to beat: Unseal the door first (the quest giver explains). Kill him, then open his chest. He rises again 20 minutes after his chest is opened.

#7 · Tikihana Jungle

The Great Tiki Kahuna

Level 23Health 8,000

He speaks for the masks of Tikihana and fights with voodoo. Another boss who must be called.

How to beat: Bring a party of level 21+. Kill his shamans and brutes before they overwhelm you.

#8 · Tikihana Jungle · behind the waterfall

The Tyrant Rex

Level 24Health 9,500

The biggest thing with teeth in the jungle, hunting by the pool beneath the Thundering Falls. The whirlpool to Atlantis is in the same pool.

How to beat: Keep moving and roll his charges. Respect the bite.

#9 · Trials of Olympus

Kronos the Stormlord

Level 26Health 12,000

Lord of the arena in the clouds. Survive the Trials of Olympus and he comes down to fight you himself, with lightning.

How to beat: Spread out so his lightning can't hit everyone at once. Bring your best gear: the Trials pay some of the biggest renown in the game.

#10 · The Molten Maze

The Ifrit

Level 27Health 9,000

Wakes behind one of the three exits of Flicker's Molten Maze. Flicker shuffles the exits every time, so you never know which door is the Ifrit's.

How to beat: If you open the wrong door, fight near the exit and roll the fire.

#11 · Dragon's Maw · the Deep Lair

Vyrthrax the Undying

Level 30Health 60,000

The raid boss of the realm: a level 30 dragon with 60,000 health. Searing Doom kills anyone who stands still, and below 10,000 health the Winged Waltz kills anyone who moves. Legendary gear goes to the top damage dealers.

How to beat: Bring ten or more. Keep moving, get behind him when he inhales, step out of fireball circles, and freeze during the Waltz. Full strategy in the How to Play guide.

Bosses you have to summon

Four bosses don't wait around: the Moonfang Alpha, Scorchtail, the Great Tiki Kahuna and the Tyrant Rex must be called, and Count Moravek's door must be unsealed. Finish each land's quest chain and the quest giver teaches you how.

Boss rewards

  • Renown: a boss kill is worth three monster kills, and a boss quest is worth ten regular quests (repeatable every 30 minutes).
  • Diamonds: every boss beyond Grubnik has a 1 in 50 chance to drop one. Vyrthrax pays 3 to every attacker still standing.
  • Gear: bosses drop rare and epic gear. Legendaries come only from the dragon.
  • Achievements and badges: every boss has its own achievement.
