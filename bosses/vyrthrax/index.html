Vyrthrax Dragon Raid Guide

Everything you need to slay the 60,000-health dragon at the bottom of the Dragon's Maw, and walk away with a legendary.

Last updated October 28, 2026 · by Luthinor Games

Play Free on Roblox

Vyrthrax the Undying is the raid boss of King or Skum?: a level 30 black dragon with 60,000 health who sleeps in the Deep Lair at the very bottom of the Dragon's Maw. He is the only source of legendary gear in the game, and the hardest fight in the realm. This guide covers how to start a raid, every attack he has, how to survive the Winged Waltz, and exactly how the loot is shared.

Who is Vyrthrax?

Every child in Lionsgate grows up hearing his name. For a hundred years he has slept beneath the Dragon's Maw, and every time he stirs the village pays for it: burned harvests, scorched roofs, whole herds carried off in the night. They call him Undying because he is. Slay him and he rises again. But every time a raid brings him down, Lionsgate gets a few years of peace, and the heroes who did it get very, very rich.

Before you go: are you ready?

  • Level 15 or higher. That's the minimum to rally a raid or follow one.
  • Green gear or better in every slot. The rally won't start without it, and you won't last without it.
  • Bank what you can't lose. Normal death rules apply in the lair. Put your best spare gear in the Royal Bank or bind it at the Soul Shrine first.
  • Bring healing. Potions and food from the Lionsgate Market. The diamond potions of Atlantis are worth it here.
  • Bring friends. Ten or more is the comfortable number. Fewer is possible. It is not comfortable.

How to start a dragon raid

  1. Press T to open quick chat and choose "Go after the dragon?"
  2. Up to 10 adventurers at level 15+ follow you as your raid party.
  3. Lead them to the Deep Lair at the bottom of the Dragon's Maw within 7 minutes, or they give up and go home.

The Dragon's Maw itself is a level 22 to 30 zone full of dragon whelps and magma drakes, so the walk down is part of the fight. Archmage Ilyra, the quest giver at the top, has work for you on the way.

The trick of the whole fight is that two of the rules pull against each other. Searing Doom kills you for standing still, and the Winged Waltz kills you for moving. Most wiped raids die in the last 10,000 health because half the party keeps walking through the Waltz, or the other half stands still too long after it.

A simple raid plan

  1. Spread out around his sides, never directly in front. Ranged fighters stay in the mid-distance and watch for fireball circles.
  2. When he inhales, everyone in front moves behind him. Then step back out to his side so the tail doesn't catch you.
  3. Call the Waltz. Below 10,000 health, the moment his wings spread, someone shouts "freeze" in chat. Nobody moves for 3 seconds.
  4. Burn him down at 20%. He's enraged. Use Strong Strike, Rend and Call to Arms, and drink potions early rather than late.

If the raid falls apart, leave. He heals back to full once the lair is empty, so a second attempt starts fresh.

Loot: who gets what

When Vyrthrax falls, his great chest in the Deep Lair glitters open and the hoard is shared by damage dealt:

  • Everyone who fought shares the gold and a big pile of XP.
  • Every attacker still standing gets 3 diamonds.
  • The top damage dealer wins Paladin's Plate Mail, a legendary chest piece.
  • The next five each get a legendary of their own.

Legendary gear is the best in the game, and the most fragile. Legendaries can never be bought back: die with one equipped, or stay logged off for more than a day, and it's gone forever. Bank it or bind it.

The Dragon's Head

The top damage dealer also takes the Dragon's Head. What happens next depends on who carries it home to Lionsgate, alive:

  • Reign of Terror: if a King, Jack or Knight brings it back, every royal gets double health for 5 minutes.
  • Do You Hear the People Sing? If anyone else brings it back, everyone except the royals gets double health for 5 minutes.

Die or log off on the way and the head is lost. Which means the walk home from the Dragon's Maw is often a second fight, against everyone who wants that head to land in different hands.

Respawn

Vyrthrax rises again 20 minutes after he falls. Every slaying also counts toward the Dragon Slayer badge.

Vyrthrax FAQ

How much health does Vyrthrax have?

60,000. He's level 30, the highest level in the game, and he gets faster when he drops to 20% health.

How many players do you need to beat Vyrthrax?

We recommend ten or more adventurers at level 15 or higher. A raid rally brings up to 10 followers with you.

What does Vyrthrax drop?

Gold and a big pile of XP for everyone, split by damage dealt, plus 3 diamonds for every attacker still standing. The top damage dealer gets Paladin's Plate Mail and the Dragon's Head, and five more legendaries go to the next five.

How long until Vyrthrax respawns?

He rises again 20 minutes after he falls. If everyone leaves or dies mid-fight, he heals back to full once the lair is empty.
