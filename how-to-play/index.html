How to Play King or Skum?: The Complete Beginner's Guide

Hello! I am the Herald, procurer of fine information!

Welcome, traveler, to the Realm. This is a land of king of the hill: everyone fights monsters, finishes quests and gathers gold to grow stronger, but only ONE may sit on the throne.

I have stood by this fountain answering questions for longer than the castle has had a roof. Everything I know is written below. Pick a chapter, or start at the top if you've never held a sword.

Chapter 1

The quick rundown

Right, the short version. I'll speak slowly.

  1. 1

    Get to work

    You start with 100 gold and a rusty sword. Fight chickens, then cows, at Sunnybrook Farm, then goblins in Greenhollow Vale. Take quests from anyone with a gold ! over their head.

  2. 2

    Grow strong

    Monsters give XP, gold and gear. Press C to see your stats and equip gear. Walk up to a glowing waystone once and you can travel there from any waystone, forever.

  3. 3

    Spend wisely

    Buy weapons, potions, arrows and more at the Lionsgate Market behind me.

  4. 4

    Know your rank

    Renown from quests, monsters and slaying other players sets your rank, from Scum up to Jack. Rank boosts or weakens your stats. Die, or stay away more than a day, and renown starts from zero.

  5. 5

    Watch the top-right panel

    The King taxes all the gold you earn or spend. Slay him to grab half his gold and plunge the realm into Anarchy. Slaying the Knight or Jack pays a bounty.

  6. 6

    Don't die

    Death costs 50% of your XP, your gear (buy it back for 10 diamonds), all your renown, your potions, ammo and quest progress. Your gold is always safe.

  7. 7

    Mind the danger zones

    The Colosseum is hardcore: lose there and the winner takes everything.

  8. 8

    Say hi!

    Press T for quick chat.

Treasure! One Treasure Chest is always hidden somewhere in the wilds, and another sits behind Count Moravek's throne. Hold the prompt for 5 seconds to open one. Getting hit interrupts you.
Bank it! Store your best gear in a vault at the Royal Bank beside the plaza. Banked items survive death and logging off. Everyone gets 3 free supply slots there for potions and ammo.
Going away? Logging off for more than a day leaves your gear behind, and the tax collector comes round with his wheelbarrow. A Writ of Absence (10 diamonds, from Mortimer Tithe by the Royal Bank) saves your gear and renown the next time.

Chapter 4

How do I become King?

XP makes you strong. Renown makes you famous. Only one of those gets you a crown.

The crown goes to whoever wins the most renown in a race. You earn it by finishing quests, a little for every monster you slay (farm animals don't count), and slaying other players: more if they out-level you, and a big chunk for the King, Jack or Knight.

  • Ten kills in a zone are worth about one of its quests. A boss is worth three kills.
  • A boss quest is worth ten regular quests, and can be redone every 30 minutes.
  • Harder quests are worth more. Farm chores are worth none; the elite bosses, the Dragon King and the Trials of Olympus are worth the most.
  • Any finished quest can be taken again for more renown, and it wins back lost XP too if you've died.
Every new King is granted 5 diamonds when crowned.

Four roads to the throne

Road one

Slay the King

The killing blow takes 50% of the King's Coffers and 3 diamonds (the rest is shared by the realm) and starts Anarchy. Nobody takes the throne by the sword alone!

Road two

Win Anarchy

For 10 minutes, whoever wins the most renown is crowned. On a brand-new server, the most renown in the first 5 minutes crowns the first King.

Road three

Be the Jack

If the King falls to monsters or logs off, the Jack inherits the throne.

Road four

Start a civil war

The Jack or Knight can turn on the King with Fennick's help. Slay the King as a rebel and the crown is yours.

The court fights as one

The King, Jack and Knight are a team. A fresh court hits hard: +50% damage against other adventurers when the King is crowned, dropping 10% every 5 minutes.

Bounties

Slay the Knight for 20% of the King's Coffers or the Jack for 30%, paid as a bounty. The vault itself isn't touched (not during Anarchy).

Greedy Kings glow

Once the tax sits at its 15% peak for 5 minutes, a trail of gold follows the King. Five minutes later a beacon of light shines down on him for the whole realm to see.

Long may he reign! A King crowned twice in a row starts a Long Reign: the tax is doubled (up to 30%), the court gets no PvP damage bonus, and the King's gold trail and beacon light up at once. The Jack wears a green trail and the Knight a blue one. Royals live at Castle Lionheart while they hold their titles.

Chapter 5

The King's Tax

Death and taxes, friend. Here we have both, and the taxes are faster.

The King takes a cut of every coin that moves: monster drops, quest rewards, fish sales and every purchase. It's taken out of the gold you earn and added on top of every price (shop prices already include it). The tax climbs the longer he reigns:

5%when crowned
10%after 5 min
15%after 10 min (peak)
30%Long Reign peak

The panel in the top right shows the tax, the coffers and the countdown.

The King's Coffers

All that tax gold has to go somewhere. It goes behind the throne.

  • King · 50%
  • Jack · 30%
  • Knight · 20%
  • An empty seat's share goes to the others.

The vault sits behind the throne in Castle Lionheart, just north of the market. The King can hold it for 5 seconds to open it and split it with the court. But opening the coffers gives up the crown and starts Anarchy. And if the King is slain first, his killer plunders the vault instead.

Chapter 8

Anarchy

Ten minutes. No Kings, no Jacks, no Peasants. I usually hide in the fountain.

10 minutes

Free-for-all

Everyone fights at normal strength and anyone can attack anyone. Golden barriers mark the safe zones: town, the chicken pen, the cow pasture and the Colosseum stands.

Gentler deaths

Keep your gear

Dying in Anarchy costs only 20% XP and you keep your gear (quest progress, renown, potions and ammo are still lost).

The finish

Most renown wins

Every quest counts. When the clock runs out, the most renown is crowned, and it carries over from before Anarchy, so a long life pays off.

Lionsgate Market is the town, and the Colosseum is hardcore PvP. Walk to each land's glowing waystone once to attune to it. After that you can travel there instantly from any waystone, and it's saved to your account.

Every land has several kinds of monster and a boss: Grubnik, Queen Skyrend, the Broodmother, Scorchtail the Magma King (the volcano in Spider Hollow), the Moonfang Alpha, Count Moravek (behind a blood-sealed door at the bottom of the crypts beneath the vampire castle), the Great Tiki Kahuna, the Tyrant Rex (under the waterfall in Tikihana), Kronos the Stormlord, and Vyrthrax the dragon, who sleeps in the Deep Lair at the bottom of the Dragon's Maw.

Monsters far below your level give little or no XP, so keep moving on as you grow.

Chapter 11

Tips for a new adventurer

Free advice, the only free thing in Lionsgate.

Chickens, then cows

Start at Sunnybrook Farm and Greenhollow Vale and do every quest you find. The cows in the pasture west of the barn are tougher and worth more XP. Farm animals stop giving XP at level 5, so move on to goblins after that.

Got milk?

About 1 cow in 20 gives Fresh Milk (+10 HP). You can carry up to 3, and Farmer Maggie wants a bottle.

Press C often

Check your stats and equip your best gear every time something drops.

Save some diamonds

Keep diamonds for buybacks. You WILL die. Better yet, soulbind your favorite gear at the Soul Shrine.

Bank what you love

Put gear you love in the Royal Bank before logging off or taking big risks.

Eat something

Food and potions from the market can save your life. And when the King's coffers get fat, the King becomes a target…

Chapter 12

How do I fight?

Steel and timing, friend. Button-mashing gets you killed out there.

Every weapon has a 4-hit combo: three swings and a finisher. Keep pressing attack to chain them.

Quick

Swords

SlashBackhandOverheadJump slash

A sword that connects knocks a foe off balance, so the whole combo lands.

Heavy

Maces & battle axes

SwingSwingSwingShield bash

The bash breaks a monster's stance for a moment.

Brutal

Hammers & great axes

Huge swingHuge swingHuge swingGround pound

The pound hits everything around you (the damage is split) and nothing interrupts it.

AttackClick or tap ⚔Chain the combo.
BlockHold Right-click or 🛡Shield only. Stops a blow completely. No shield? Everything costs 15% less stamina instead.
RollShift or 🌀You can't be hurt mid-roll. Roll through glowing circles, breath and big swings.
Shoulder cameraCtrl or Right ShiftLocks the camera over your shoulder. Your swings follow the camera.

Perfect guard

Blocking costs stamina and heavy blows break your guard. Watch for the red flash before a big swing. Raise your guard just before the hit lands: no cost, and the monster reels.

Stamina

The yellow bar under your health. Swings, rolls and blocked hits drain it; stop for a breath and it comes back. Empty it and you're exhausted for 5 seconds.

Pick your moments

Getting hit ends your combo. Monsters rest after a flurry: that's your opening. Some dodge, some block, the clever ones parry. Break their stance for extra damage.

Against players: swing at the same moment and your blades CLASH. Mace and hammer finishers smash through shields.

How do I earn gold?

Nothing is free here. Except my advice. And the fountain water, probably.

Coming in

Where gold comes from

You start with 100 gold, enough for a fishing rod and a few basics. Earn more from monster drops, quest rewards, Treasure Chests, salvaging gear and fishing. You can also buy gold (and diamonds) with Robux from Treasurer Opaline at the Royal Treasury, or the button on the left of your screen.

Going out

Where gold goes

The Lionsgate Market behind me sells weapons, bows and staffs, potions, food, hats and capes, ammo and more. Walk up to a vendor and press the prompt to shop. And remember: the King's Tax takes a cut of all of it.

Chapter 21

Civil war

Only the Jack or the Knight can start one. Everyone else gets told to come back when they have something to lose.

Talk to Fennick the coin-flipper behind the Thieves' Guild (never in the first 5 minutes of a reign). The other royal gets 10 seconds, unharmed, to join the rebellion or stand with the King. No answer means the King.

  • Rebels see an arrow pointing to the King for the whole war. Everyone else can still hunt them.
  • The King puts a bounty on each rebel that grows as the coffers fill. Slay a rebel Jack and your renown jumps 20 above the next highest; a rebel Knight, 10.
  • While the war lasts, nobody can overtake a rebel's title by renown.
  • A rebel who logs off has fled, and their renown is gone when they return.
  • A rebel who falls is dragged to the Stockade by the castle and pelted with tomatoes, then dropped to the bottom of the realm.
Victory: a rebel who slays the King takes the throne and 100% of the coffers (half each if the other royal joined), plus 20 diamonds, and 10 for a royal who joined. The new King's renown is set 30 above the realm's highest.

Chapter 22

Can other players attack me?

It's the Crown versus the People. Pick a side. Well, your rank picks it for you.

Crown vs. People

Royals can attack any commoner, and any commoner can attack a royal. Royals can't hurt each other, and commoners can't hurt each other. Band together to bring the King down!

Anarchy & the Colosseum

During Anarchy it's a free-for-all everywhere except inside the golden barriers. In the Colosseum it's always a free-for-all.

After you respawn you get 8 seconds of protection, which ends early if you attack someone. Stay alert, keep your health up, and don't carry gear you can't afford to lose.

Chapter 23

The Colosseum

West of here stands the Colosseum. I do not recommend it. I do recommend the seats.

Hardcore PvP. Inside, anyone can attack anyone, even King against Jack. If you die, the player who killed you takes ALL your gear, even legendaries, and you lose ALL your experience, back to level 1. You keep your gold. There's no buying it back.

Want to watch instead? Climb the outer staircase to the spectator stands. Nobody can hit across the arena walls.

Chapter 25

What happens when I die?

Death is costly here. I say that kindly, and from a safe distance.

You lose

  • 50% of your XP. You can drop levels!
  • All your gear. Buy it back for 10 diamonds (only your LAST death can be bought back).
  • All quest progress: kill counts and collections start over.
  • Your renown, down to zero.
  • Potions, food, flasks and ammo.
  • 40% of your fishing progress.
  • Legendary gear, forever. It can never be bought back.

You keep

  • Your gold. Always.
  • Anything in your Royal Bank vault and supply slots.
  • Gear bound to your soul at the Soul Shrine (the binding is spent).
  • Honor and assassin gear, which are account-bound.
During Anarchy you only lose 20% XP. Monsters can't take your gear then, but if another player kills you, they take it and buyback costs 500 gold instead of diamonds.
Going away for a while? Mercy rule: log back in within 24 hours and you keep everything. Staying logged off longer than a day leaves your unbound, unbanked gear behind, and buying it back costs 10 diamonds. A Writ of Absence (10 diamonds) from Mortimer Tithe by the Royal Bank saves your gear and renown the next time you're gone.

The buyback button appears on the left of your screen.

Chapter 26

Treasure Chests

One is always out there. It could be anywhere. It could be behind you.

The wandering chest

One chest is always hidden somewhere in the wilds. Only the Treasure Hunter class sees its golden beam from afar and is told which land it's in. Everyone else has to get lucky.

Opening it

Hold the prompt for 5 seconds. Anything that hits you interrupts it, so clear the area or bring a friend. Inside: 50 gold and a piece of the best gear that land can drop. Then a new one appears elsewhere.

The Count's chest

A second chest sits at the back of Count Moravek's chamber. His Blood Sucker breaks your grip while he lives. He rises again 20 minutes after his death, but only once his chest has been opened.

Vyrthrax shares his hoard with everyone who fought him the moment he falls: his great chest in the Deep Lair simply glitters open.

Chapter 29

Vyrthrax the Undying

*lowers his voice* Every child in Lionsgate grows up hearing his name.

For a hundred years he has slept in the Deep Lair, at the very bottom of the Dragon's Maw, and every time he stirs our village pays for it. Burned harvests, scorched roofs, whole herds carried off in the night. Farmer Maggie still won't talk about the year he took her father's barn.

We call him Undying because he is. Slay him and he rises again. But every time brave souls bring him down, the village gets a few years of peace, and the heroes get very, very rich.

Level 30Raid boss
60,000Health
10+Adventurers recommended
20 minUntil he rises again

How to defeat him

Rally a raid

At level 15 in green gear or better in every slot, press T and say “Go after the dragon?” Up to 10 level-15+ adventurers follow you. Reach the Deep Lair within 7 minutes or they give up.

Keep moving

The lair is cursed with Searing Doom: every second you stand still it stacks and burns. At 10 stacks you die. Moving burns it off.

Fire breath

A deep breath means fire in a huge cone in front of him. Get behind him when he inhales…

…but not too far

He swipes his tail at anyone at his back and knocks them flying. His wing buffet slams away everyone close; get back in.

Fireballs

Fighters far away get fireballs. Step out of the glowing circle.

The Winged Waltz

Below 10,000 health: when he spreads his wings you get a 3-second warning, then for 3 seconds anyone who MOVES dies. Freeze, wait, then move again before Searing Doom gets you.

At 20% health he becomes ENRAGED and attacks much faster. If everyone leaves or dies, he heals back to full once the lair is empty.

The reward

The hoard

Split by damage dealt. Everyone shares gold and a big pile of XP, and every attacker still standing gets 3 diamonds. The top damage dealer wins Paladin's Plate Mail, and five more legendaries go to the top five.

Reign of Terror

The top damage dealer takes the Dragon's Head. If a King, Jack or Knight carries it back to town alive, the royals get double health for 5 minutes.

Do You Hear the People Sing?

If anyone else brings the head home, everyone but the royals gets double health for 5 minutes. Die or log off on the way and the head is lost.
