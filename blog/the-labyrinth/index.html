The Labyrinth: A Complete Guide to the Sphinx, the Maze and the Minotaur King

A maze in the jungle, a Sphinx at the door, and a Minotaur King with a lightning axe and a disco floor. Here's how to get in, get through and get rich.

By Luthinor Games, with commentary from Herald Bartholomew

Beyond the ridge on the eastern edge of the Tikihana Jungle there is a maze nobody built on purpose. Its walls are sandstone, its corridors twist back on themselves, and at its heart, on a floor that lights up like a nightclub, waits the Minotaur King. He has an axe made of lightning, 40,000 health and absolutely no sense of humor about his hoard.

This is everything you need to know to get in, get through and get rich.

Finding the Sphinx

The way in is guarded by the Sphinx, Keeper of the Labyrinth, who sits in a golden shrine at the eastern edge of the Tikihana Jungle (past the raptors, past the waterfall, keep going). Walk up to her and press E to speak (on a phone or tablet, tap the Speak button that appears just above your abilities). The nameplate gives you fair warning: the Labyrinth is built for heroes of level 25 to 30.

Getting in

  • Your first walk is free. After that, a run costs 1,000 gold or 10 diamonds.
  • Go alone or bring your party, up to five heroes. A party pays the toll once and shares the run.
  • Party members who are far away get an invitation. When the leader enters, everyone else in the party gets a "The Labyrinth calls!" window with a 45-second countdown. Press Join them and the sands carry you straight in.
  • One band at a time. Only one run can happen per server. If another group is inside, the Sphinx will tell you to wait until they return (or fall).
  • You have 30 minutes. A timer at the top of your screen counts down. When the sands run out, the Labyrinth casts you out.
  • No royals. The King, the Jack and the Knights can't enter, and can't follow their party in. The throne needs its court. Lay down your title first, highness.

Inside the maze

The Labyrinth is a seventeen-by-seventeen grid of corridors with the Minotaur King's arena at the center. The beasts that live here know the way through the halls and will hunt you down them.

MonsterLevelWhat to watch for
Bone Sentinel25 to 27Rattling skeleton guards. The weakest of the three, but there are a lot of them.
Gorgon26 to 28Fast, poisonous bites, and a spinning Serpent Lash that hits everything around her. She dodges, too.
Labyrinth Bull27 to 29The toughest beast in the halls. Watch for the Horn Gore and the spinning Bull Rage.

Hidden in the dead ends are treasure chests: seven small green chests and five large blue ones. Small chests hold an uncommon item; large chests hold a rare one. Each chest holds one prize, for whoever opens it, so if you're in a party, decide your spoils fairly. The Sphinx suggests rolling for them: press T and choose Roll, or type /roll in chat.

The Minotaur King

Step through the horned gate at the center of the maze and the Minotaur King arrives in a crash of lightning. He's a level 30 elite with 40,000 health, a crown, and a double-headed axe crackling with electricity.

He has four moves you need to know:

  • Lightning Axe. He winds up and swings. If it lands, the lightning arcs to two more heroes nearby. Stand back from him and he hurls a bolt at the spot where you're standing instead; when you see the blue circle under your feet, move.
  • Labrys Cleave. A huge overhead swing that hits the whole area around where it lands.
  • The Stomp. A red ring appears around him with a five-second countdown. When it hits zero, everyone on the ground inside the ring takes a massive hit. Jump it, roll through it, or just get out of the ring.
  • The Lightning Dance. His big one. Read on.

The Lightning Dance

The arena floor isn't just floor. It's a light-up disco board: an 8-by-8 grid of glowing squares that chase colors around while you fight. When the Minotaur King drops to 80%, 55% and 30% health, he rises into the air, shouts "LIGHTNING DANCE!", and the whole floor starts flashing.

When the flashing stops, the board freezes. A handful of squares glow green and the rest go dark red. You have ten seconds to get onto a green square. When the countdown ends, the storm strikes every hero who isn't standing on one.

While he's riding the storm he can't be hurt, so don't waste the ten seconds swinging at him. Find your green square, stand on it, and wait for the "SAFE!" over your head.

The Minotaur King's hoard

Bring him down and a giant chest appears at the heart of the arena. Every hero in the run can open it for their own share:

  • 10 diamonds
  • 1,000 gold
  • A random purple (epic) item

The hoard is once a day. Open it and the Labyrinth is closed to you for 24 hours; the Sphinx will tell you exactly how long you have left if you try to sneak back in early. Your party can still go in without you, but you'll have to sit this one out.

When you're done, step into the ring of golden sands beside the hoard and it carries you back to the Sphinx.

Tips from the Herald

  • Bring friends. A party pays the toll once, and the Lightning Axe has a harder time picking on any one of you.
  • Clear the halls on the way in. Every Gorgon and Bull you leave behind is one that follows you into the arena.
  • Don't stand in a clump. The Lightning Axe arcs from hero to hero.
  • Time the Stomp. Jump just as the countdown runs out and you'll be in the air when it lands.
  • Know where the green is. When the Dance starts, stop attacking and get ready to move. Ten seconds goes fast when you're on the wrong side of the floor.
  • Watch the clock. Thirty minutes is plenty, unless you spend twenty of them lost.
  • Falling is final. If you go down in the maze, your run is over and the realm's usual death rules apply.

"I asked the Sphinx why the Minotaur King has a disco floor. She said it was a riddle. I asked what the answer was. She said, and I quote, 'Dance.'" — Herald Bartholomew
