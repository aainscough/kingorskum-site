Patch Notes: The Monster Overhaul, Real Bear and Lion Forms, and a New Opening

Every monster in the realm has been rebuilt, the Druid's Talismans now turn you into a real bear or lion, and new players get a proper cinematic welcome.

By Luthinor Games, with commentary from Herald Bartholomew

The great monster overhaul

Every monster in the realm has been rebuilt from the ground up, except the goblins (they were already perfect, and they'd never let us hear the end of it). Spiders, scorpions, raptors, the Tyrant Rex, hippogryphs, the Ifrit, vampires, the Magma Fiends, the Tikis, the Storm Titan, Kronos, Count Moravek and even the cow all have sharp new low-poly models.

  • Attacks you can read. Monsters now coil back before they strike and lunge into the blow, so you can see a hit coming and roll out of the way.
  • They feel your hits. Land a blow and the monster flinches.
  • Smoother fights. The new models are much lighter, so busy fights run faster.

Werewolves: leaner, shaggier, scarier

The Werewolf, Shadowfang and the Moonfang Alpha were looking a little too much like walking barrels. They now have huge shoulders and a narrow waist, a hunched, prowling stance, and a thick coat of shaggy fur from the mane to the wrists. Their claws are longer, too. You have been warned.

The Druid's Talismans: become a real bear or lion

The talismans sold by Archdruid Coralynn in the Lost City of Atlantis used to give you a fur coat. Now they turn you into the real animal: a big brown bear or a lion with a full mane, walking on four legs. Your armor and weapon disappear from view, but they still count: every stat, every bit of damage, every special.

Each animal fights its own way. The bear swipes with its paws and finishes with a Bear Slam: it rears up on its hind legs and comes crashing down on everything around it. The lion claws, bites and finishes with a leaping Pounce. You can still roll, and your four level abilities become the animal's own:

KeyBearLion
Q (level 10)Maul: your next blow deals double damagePounce: leap at your prey, your next blow deals double damage
R (level 15)Rending Claws: your next blow makes the target bleedSavage Bite: your next blow makes the target bleed
F (level 20)Thunderous Roar: +3 all stats for 20 seconds and stuns nearby monstersKing's Roar: +3 all stats for 20 seconds and slows nearby monsters
G (level 25)Iron Hide: take 50% less damage for 5 secondsGolden Mane: take 50% less damage for 5 seconds

Yes, they growl and roar. Atlantis wares still vanish when you die, so have the Warlock bind your talisman to your soul.

A cinematic welcome

New players no longer get a wall of text. The opening is now a short cinematic that flies you around the realm and shows you the throne, the King's Royal Coffers and how the crown changes hands, with subtitles that type out at the bottom of the screen. Already know it all? Hit Skip.

A fairer crown

You have to be here to rule. If the crown would pass to you while you're away (in the Labyrinth, a portal, a sky boss arena, your house, the volcano or the Genie's lair), it goes to the most renowned player who is actually in the realm.

Heading into the Labyrinth? It got party invites and a lot more in this update, so it has its own write-up now: The Labyrinth: A Complete Guide to the Sphinx, the Maze and the Minotaur King.

One more thing

The realm has edges. People keep falling off them. We've heard reports that if you end up far enough outside the world, things start to look... yellow. The Herald refuses to comment.

The crown is waiting.
