The Making of King or Skum?: From an Empty Baseplate to Launch Day

208 saves, 53 published updates and one very busy launch day. Here's the whole timeline of how the realm was built, with pictures.

By Luthinor Games, with commentary from Herald Bartholomew

King or Skum? didn't exist at the start of October. Three weeks later it has a kingdom, a throne that everyone is trying to steal, eleven bosses, a dragon, a drowned city and a website. Roblox keeps a record of every time a game is saved and every time it's published, so we pulled ours and turned it into a timeline. These are real numbers: 208 saves and 53 published updates between October 10 and October 28, 2026.

October 10–11: Laying the first stone

104 saves · 0 published

The first two days were all building and no publishing: 104 saves before a single player could join. The idea came first, from the card game Scum: every player holds a rank from King down to Skum, and your rank changes how strong you are. Everything else in the game grew out of that one rule.

A game about a throne needs a kingdom around it: Lionsgate, with its fountain and market street, and Castle Lionheart on the hill with a golden throne inside.

October 12–14: The first players walk in

61 saves · 14 published updates

On October 12 the game was published for the first time, and the updates started flowing out: six on the first day, then four a day after that. From here on, players could see the realm grow between visits. A kingdom needs somewhere dangerous to go, and the lands beyond the walls kept filling up with monsters, quest givers and bosses, from the chickens of Sunnybrook Farm and the goblins of Greenhollow Vale outward.

October 15–18: The big build

31 saves · 29 published updates

This was the busiest stretch: 29 published updates in four days, including 13 on October 15 and 14 on October 18. Across the first weeks, the realm grew outward and downward until it had everything that makes King or Skum? feel like King or Skum?: the King's Tax and Coffers, Anarchy, the Colosseum, the Royal Bank, the White Tower classes and a boss in every land, all the way to the dragon.

It also picked up some of its strangest corners: a whole city at the bottom of a whirlpool, a genie in a lamp, and a dog in a top hat who lives in a tree.

October 24–27: Polish and the road to launch

6 saves · 4 published updates

After the big build, the work slowed down and got careful: fewer updates, more testing, and getting everything ready for real players.

October 28: Launch day

6 published updates · and a website

Launch day was the biggest single day of work since the big build, and it was all about bringing people in and giving them a reason to stay.

  • Roblox badges. Achievements now award real badges on your Roblox profile, and older achievements are awarded the next time you visit. Full patch notes →
  • Loyal Subjects. Joining the Luthinor Games group pays 30 diamonds.
  • Bring a friend. After the realm's big moments, the game offers to invite your friends.
  • Sir Reginald's vine now climbs to his branch in Tikihana, and the Goblintown gate opens for anyone in Snik's goblin disguise.

kingorskum.com went live the same day: the story of the realm, the Herald's full How to Play guide, every boss and land, and this blog.

Then came codes. The Herald, the Royal Bank and the Royal Treasury all now open the Royal Code Scroll. The launch code LONGLIVETHEKING pays 100 diamonds to the first 1,000 adventurers, and the codes page counts down how many are left, live from the game servers.

Three more rewards are already built into the game and unlock by themselves as the realm grows: the Royal Angler's Rod at 100,000 visits, an Alchemist's Still for your home at 500,000, and at 1,000,000 visits a royal purple vanity set: a King's Garb and a Queen's Gown, collected from Mirabel Finch at the Silken Spool. Wear one while you're King and the King's Tax rises by 5 points for as long as it's on.

The last fixes of the day were small but visible: Atlantis got its own music, mystical and underwater, and Mortimer Tithe's wheelbarrow stopped floating beside the Royal Bank.

What's next

The realm is open, but it isn't finished. Next up: more badges, pages for every boss and land, and whatever the first thousand players break. Every update will be posted on the updates page first, and the big ones get a write-up here.

Thank you for being here at the start. Long live the King. For now.

The crown is waiting.
