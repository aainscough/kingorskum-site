Patch Notes: Badges, a Gift for Loyal Subjects, and Bringing Friends

Your achievements now earn real Roblox badges, joining the group pays 30 diamonds, and it just got easier to drag your friends into the realm.

By Luthinor Games, with commentary from Herald Bartholomew

Achievements are now Roblox badges

Earn an achievement in King or Skum? and you'll get the matching Roblox badge on your profile. The first badges are live: King Me, Dragon Slayer, The One True King, Loyal Subject, and one for a certain blue fellow who lives at the bottom of the world. More are being forged every day.

Already earned some of these before badges existed? You'll be awarded them automatically the next time you join.

Loyal Subject: 30 diamonds for joining the group

Join the Luthinor Games group and you'll earn the Loyal Subject achievement and 30 diamonds, once per account. Look for the Join Group button in the bottom-left corner of your screen. If you're already a member, the reward is paid the moment you log in.

Bring your friends

King of the Hill is better with a full server, so after the realm's big moments (a coronation, a dethroning, a certain lamp) the game will offer to invite your friends. It won't nag: once every couple of minutes at most.

Smaller changes

  • A vine up to Sir Reginald. The border walls made the old route impossible, so there's now a vine on the jungle floor beneath his tree. Press E to climb. He has been very patient.
  • Goblintown's gate now swings open for anyone wearing Snik's goblin disguise. Humies remain unwelcome.
  • Loyal Subject is listed under The Realm in your achievements, so you can see if you've claimed it.

Why we added badges

Badges show up on your Roblox profile, which means the things you've done in the realm now follow you around. Slay the dragon and everyone who looks at your profile knows. We're adding badges for the rest of the achievements over the coming days, starting with the big diamond rewards and the secret ones. You can see every achievement and what it pays in the Achievements tab of the Character panel (press C).

What to do with 30 free diamonds

Thirty diamonds is enough for three gear buybacks, three Writs of Absence, or most of the way to a Tier 2 vault at the Royal Bank. If you're new, we'd save them for buybacks: you will die, and getting your gear back for 10 diamonds is the best deal in the realm. More ideas on the diamonds guide and the codes page.

The crown is waiting.
