Characters and NPCs of King or Skum?

Who's who in Lionsgate and beyond, what each character wants from you, and where to find them.

Last updated October 28, 2026 · by Luthinor Games

Play Free on Roblox

Lionsgate is full of people who want something from you: your gold, your gear, your company, or occasionally your soul. Here's who's who in King or Skum?, and where to find them.

The folk of the realm

Herald of the Realm · by the fountain

Herald Bartholomew

Answers every question a new adventurer has ever asked, usually at length. His full guide lives on the How to Play page.

Royal Tax Collector · by the Royal Bank

Mortimer Tithe

Stay away more than a day and Mortimer wheels your gear off. He also sells the Writ of Absence (10 diamonds) that keeps him away.

Sunnybrook Farm · first quest giver

Farmer Maggie

Your first quests. She has chickens, cows and a long memory of the year the dragon took her father's barn.

The Royal Bank

Banker Goldwyn

Opens vaults where gear survives death. Everyone gets 3 free supply slots.

Guildmaster · The Crooked Dagger

Ruffus

Runs the Thieves' Guild and pays Dark Tokens for slain royals: 1 per Knight, 3 per Jack, 5 per King.

Behind the Thieves' Guild

Fennick the Coin-Flipper

Helps the Jack or a Knight start a civil war against the King. Everyone else is told to come back when they have something to lose.

Keeper of the Veil · the Spirit Realm

Seraphine

The first face you see after your first death. She explains the curse of dying, and sends you back lighter.

The Soul Shrine · by the fountain

Morwen the Soulbinder

Binds an item to your soul so it survives your next death. Common items cost gold; rare and up cost diamonds.

The fire sprite · Ashen Caldera

Flicker

Giggles at the foot of the volcano and dares heroes into the Molten Maze. One exit has 5 diamonds. One has the Ifrit.

Enjoyer of small talk · Tikihana

Sir Reginald Woofington

A gentleman dog at the top of a jungle tree. Climb the vine and talk about the weather. He has never asked anyone to slay anything.

Location unknown

The Genie of the Hollow

Some say if you fall far enough out of the world, someone at the bottom grants wishes. Read the small print.

Other quest givers

Every land has its own quest giver with a chain of jobs: Marshal Brennan in Greenhollow Vale, Skyrider Tamsin in the Hippogryph Highlands, Brother Aldous and Ember-Seer Kaida in Spider Hollow, Archmage Ilyra at the Dragon's Maw and more. Look for the gold ! over their heads. Snik the Outcast, hiding near Goblintown, sells the goblin disguise that opens Goblintown's gate.

In town

Grandmaster Aurelion sells classes in the White Tower. Treasurer Opaline runs the Royal Treasury. Tobias Wick sells fishing rods and buys your fish. The Lawmaker stands in the throne room, waiting for the King's next decree.
