Every Secret and Easter Egg in King or Skum?

Hints first, spoilers behind a click. Every hidden character, secret achievement and easter egg in the realm.

Last updated October 28, 2026 · by Luthinor Games

Play Free on Roblox

King or Skum? hides a few things that aren't on any map. We'll give you hints first, and full spoilers behind a click, so you can choose how much you want to know. Most of them pay diamonds, and each has its own achievement and badge.

Secret achievements

You've Never Had a Friend Like Me (5 diamonds)

Hint: some say if you fall far enough out of the world, someone at the bottom grants wishes.

Spoiler: there's a gap behind the rocks near Goblintown. Get stuck back there, fall, and wait. A lamp appears, and the Genie of the Hollow offers you three wishes. Choose carefully. Royals who fall are sent straight back to the castle.

Diamond Tax Man (5 diamonds)

Hint: try to out-glitch a wish. The taxman always collects.

Spoiler: if you wish to be rich and then try to log off before the Genie takes his diamonds back, he finds you anyway. Try it twice and Mortimer Tithe arrives with his wheelbarrow.

Small Talk with Pups (1 diamond)

Hint: a very good listener lives somewhere high up.

Spoiler: Sir Reginald Woofington sits at the top of a tall tree in Tikihana Jungle. Climb the vine from the jungle floor and talk to him five times.

As-Real as can be (3 diamonds)

Hint: in the far corner of the realm, something is smiling at you. Keep talking.

Spoiler: a cheerful yellow flower named ??? grows in the far corner of the map. Talk to it three times. It doesn't stay cheerful.

Easter eggs

  • The chickens remember. Every 100th chicken you slay, the flock comes for you. It's harmless. Mostly.
  • Goblintown's gate says No Humies, and means it. Snik's disguise gets you in.
  • The Comedy Hut in Goblintown has seats for an audience. Nobody asks how good the jokes are.
  • The Most Crowned board outside Castle Lionheart ranks everyone who has worn the crown.
  • Past Kings wear a tiny crown over their heads with the number of times they've ruled.

The One True King

The rarest achievement in the game: slay every boss of the realm, then take the crown, all without dying once (logging off is fine). It pays 500 diamonds and the permanent title The One True King. See the boss list for what you're up against.

Go Find Them YourselfNo map. No guide. Just you and a very suspicious flower.
