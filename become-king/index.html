How to Become King in King or Skum? (4 Ways)

Every Roblox server of King or Skum? crowns one King. Here's how the crown is won, four ways to take it, and how to hold it once everyone is coming for you.

Last updated October 28, 2026 · by Luthinor Games

Play Free on Roblox

In King or Skum?, every Roblox server crowns exactly one King. The King gets the biggest stat boost in the game (×1.5), taxes every coin that moves, and commands the town guards. Here's every way to get the crown, and how to keep it.

Renown: the only currency that matters

The crown goes to whoever wins the most renown. XP makes you strong; renown makes you famous. You earn it by:

  • Finishing quests. Harder quests pay more. Farm chores pay none; the elite bosses, the Dragon and the Trials of Olympus pay the most.
  • Slaying monsters. A little per kill, the same for everyone (farm animals don't count). Ten kills in a zone are worth about one of its quests.
  • Slaying bosses. A boss is worth three kills, and a boss quest is worth ten regular quests. Boss quests can be redone every 30 minutes.
  • Slaying players. More if they out-level you, and a big chunk for the King, Jack or Knight.
  • Repeating quests. Any finished quest can be taken again for renown, and wins back lost XP if you've died.
Renown drops to zero when you die or stay away for more than a day. Staying alive is how you climb.

The four roads to the throne

1. Slay the King

The killing blow takes 50% of the King's Coffers and 3 diamonds, and the rest is shared by the realm. But nobody takes the throne by the sword alone: killing the King starts Anarchy.

2. Win Anarchy

For 10 minutes there are no ranks and it's a free-for-all outside the golden safe zones. When the clock runs out, whoever has the most renown is crowned. Renown carries over from before Anarchy, so a long life pays off. Anarchy also starts when a King opens his own coffers, and on a brand-new server the most renown in the first 5 minutes crowns the first King.

3. Be the Jack

The Jack is second in line. If the King dies to monsters or logs off, the Jack inherits the throne without a fight.

4. Start a civil war

The Jack or a Knight can turn on the King by talking to Fennick the coin-flipper behind the Thieves' Guild (never in the first 5 minutes of a reign). A rebel who slays the King takes the throne and 100% of the coffers, plus 20 diamonds.

How to become King fast

  1. Join a fresh server. On a new server the first King is crowned after just 5 minutes, and the competition has no renown banked yet.
  2. Chain quests in the highest zone you can survive. Harder quests pay more renown per minute than farming kills.
  3. Redo boss quests every 30 minutes. One boss quest is worth ten regular ones.
  4. Don't die. Bring food and potions, bank your best gear, and avoid fights you can't win. One death wipes everything.
  5. Time your strike. Watch the coffers in the top-right panel. When the King is fat and the tax is high, everyone will be hunting him. Be the one holding the most renown when Anarchy ends.

How to keep the crown

  • Lean on your court. The King, Jack and Knight fight as one team, and a fresh court hits for +50% damage against other adventurers, dropping 10% every 5 minutes.
  • Mind the tax. Once the tax has sat at its 15% peak for 5 minutes, a gold trail follows you, and 5 minutes later a beacon of light shows the whole realm where you are.
  • Use your decree. Visit the Lawmaker for one decree per reign: make everyone bow, call a Championship, or release the Golden Hare.
  • Go for a Long Reign. Get crowned twice in a row and the tax doubles (up to 30%), though the court loses its damage bonus and all three royals glow.

How Kings lose the crown

A King loses the throne when he's slain, when he opens the coffers (the court splits the gold 50/30/20 and Anarchy begins), when he logs off, or when a rebel wins a civil war. Every King falls eventually. The oath of the first King says so.
