King or Skum? Map: Every Zone in the Realm

Every land in King or Skum?, in the order you should explore them, with levels, monsters, bosses and the hidden places most players never find.

Last updated October 28, 2026 · by Luthinor Games

Play Free on Roblox

The realm of King or Skum? is one big connected world on Roblox, with the market town of Lionsgate in the middle and each land getting more dangerous the farther you go. Walk to a land's glowing waystone once and you can teleport back to it from any other waystone, forever.

Explore the realm

Town · safe zone

Lionsgate

The capital. The market, the Royal Bank, the Royal Treasury, the White Tower of classes, the Soul Shrine, Castle Lionheart and the Herald by the fountain. Every trade here pays the King's Tax.

Level 1

Sunnybrook Farm

Chickens, cows and Farmer Maggie. Where every adventure starts. Farm animals stop giving XP at level 5, and about 1 cow in 20 gives Fresh Milk.

Levels 2 to 7

Greenhollow Vale & Goblintown

Goblin scrappers and thugs, hollow creepers and Grubnik the Rotten. Goblintown's gate only opens for adventurers in Snik's goblin disguise.

Levels 8 to 13

Hippogryph Highlands

Hippogryphs and thunderbeaks swoop over the Temple of Skies, ruled by Queen Skyrend.

Levels 8 to 15

Spider Hollow & the Ashen Caldera

Giant spiders, the Brood Tree and the Broodmother, and a volcano with Scorchtail inside and Flicker's Molten Maze beneath.

Levels 13 to 20

Moonfang Woods & Castle Moravek

Werewolves and shadowfangs, the Moonfang Alpha, and the vampire castle whose crypts hide Count Moravek and his chest.

Levels 17 to 24

Tikihana Jungle & the Thundering Falls

Raptors, tiki warriors, shamans and brutes, the Great Tiki Kahuna, and the Tyrant Rex by the waterfall pool. Sir Reginald Woofington lives up a tree here.

Hidden · diamond shops

Atlantis

A drowned city reached through a whirlpool beneath the Thundering Falls. Six shops that take only diamonds, including druid talismans that turn you into a bear or a lion.

Levels 20 to 26

Stormcrown Peaks & the Acropolis

Storm titans guard the stair to the Acropolis, and the Trials of Olympus wait in the arena in the clouds above, ending with Kronos the Stormlord.

Levels 22 to 30 · raid

Dragon's Maw

Dragon whelps, magma drakes, Archmage Ilyra, and at the very bottom, the Deep Lair of Vyrthrax the Undying.

Hardcore PvP

The Colosseum

West of town. Inside, anyone can attack anyone, and the winner takes all your gear and XP. Spectators watch safely from the stands.

Monsters far below your level give little or no XP, so keep moving outward as you grow. See the boss list for every tyrant's level and health.
