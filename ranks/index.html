King or Skum? Ranks Explained: Skum to King

Your rank in King or Skum? multiplies every stat you have. Here's what each rank does, how the court is built, and how to climb from Skum to King.

Last updated October 28, 2026 · by Luthinor Games

Play Free on Roblox

Every player on a King or Skum? server holds a rank, and your rank multiplies all of your stats. Ranks are set by renown, except the King, who keeps his crown until he's slain, opens the coffers or leaves.

RankStat multiplierWhat it means
King×1.5One per server. Taxes every coin, commands the guards, proclaims decrees, lives in Castle Lionheart. Hunted by everyone.
Jack×1.25The King's right hand and heir. Inherits the throne if the King falls to monsters or logs off. Slaying him pays a 30% bounty.
Knight×1.1Sword of the court. Fights alongside the King. Slaying a Knight pays a 20% bounty.
Worker×1An honest adventurer with normal stats.
Peasant×0.9Slightly weaker than normal.
Skum×0.8The bottom of the realm. Every legend starts here.

How the court grows with the server

Not every server has every rank. The court is sized to the number of players:

  • 1 to 5 players: just a King.
  • 6 to 10 players: a King and a Skum.
  • 11 to 20 players: King, Jack, Peasant and Skum.
  • 21+ players: the full court, with Knights and Workers.

The list labelled THE REALM in the top-right corner of your screen shows everyone's rank and renown.

The Crown versus the People

The King, Jack and Knight are one team. Royals can attack any commoner, and any commoner can attack a royal, but royals can't hurt each other and commoners can't hurt each other. That makes every server a story of the many against the few. A freshly crowned court hits for +50% damage against other adventurers, falling by 10% every 5 minutes.

Why the name: King or Skum?

The ranks come from the card game Scum (also known as President or Kings and Scum), where the winner of each round becomes King and the loser becomes Scum, and the next round starts unfair on purpose. In King or Skum?, the unfairness is the point: the King is stronger, richer and protected, and the only way to fix that is to take the crown yourself.

How to rank up fast

  1. Finish quests in the hardest zone you can survive.
  2. Redo boss quests every 30 minutes.
  3. Slay royals: the King, Jack and Knight are worth big renown and bounties.
  4. Don't die. Renown resets to zero.
